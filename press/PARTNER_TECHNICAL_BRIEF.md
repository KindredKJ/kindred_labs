## Executive summary

Kindred Labs researches the intersection of agentic execution, persistent computational state, cryptographically authenticated data transport, and interactive experiences. The current collaboration focus is to strengthen independently measured engineering results and move selected products toward usable, commercial integrations.

**Evidence milestone:** [Strata Data Port Network PR #40](https://github.com/KindredKJ/strata-data-port-network/pull/40), merged October 28, 2026, records 16/16 checks by a standalone third-environment verifier for one signed and bounded cross-provider `HASH_TEXT` recovery artifact. The verifier checks a pinned Ed25519 signature, checkpoint hashes and metadata, and a recomputed signed output hash.

**Limits:** This does not establish automatic failover, abrupt power-loss recovery, physical-machine attestation, proven anonymous access to the public endpoint, or general production readiness. Neither the artifact nor this brief proves paying customers, revenue or independent endorsement.

## Concrete requests

1. **Engineering:** a technical owner, architecture review, platform integration guidance, compatibility requirements and benchmark feedback.
2. **Resources:** startup cloud/GPU/API credits, eligible grants, hardware discounts and testing infrastructure.
3. **Capital:** introductions to eligible seed-stage investors or active venture formation programs for appropriately scoped products.
4. **Go-to-market:** published case-study consideration, developer showcase, technical talk, community distribution and joint launch **only after written agreement and successful verification**.
5. **Commercial:** identify one sellable use case per collaboration and agree on requirements, ownership, support model and next milestones before public claims.
